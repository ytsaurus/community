# YTsaurus Community Chat Policy

## 1. Purpose  
This community chat exists to facilitate knowledge sharing, peer support, and discussion among users. The participation is voluntary and subject to the principles outlined below.

---

## 2. Best-Effort Support  
- **Voluntary Nature**: Responses in this chat are provided **on a best-effort basis**.  
- **No Guarantees**: While the team strives to address questions in a **reasonable timeframe**, no guarantees of response time or accuracy are provided.  
- **No SLA**: This chat does not offer any formal Service Level Agreement (SLA). For critical or urgent inquiries, please reach out to official support channels.  

---

## 3. Code of Conduct  

### 3.1 Our Commitment  
We are committed to fostering a diverse, inclusive, and respectful community. By participating, you agree to uphold a safe and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of experience, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, age, religion, nationality, or any other defining characteristic.  

### 3.2 Expected Behavior  
- Be welcoming and kind.  
- Show patience and understanding.  
- Respect differences of opinion and experiences.  
- Be mindful of your tone and wording to avoid miscommunication.  

### 3.3 Unacceptable Behavior  
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:  
- **Discrimination or offensive conduct**: This includes sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise harmful speech or actions.  
- **Harassment**: Intimidation, unwanted attention, or any behavior that causes discomfort to others.  
- **Disrespect**: This includes dismissive attitudes, inappropriate jokes, or ridiculing others' contributions.  
- **Unsolicited promotions**: Avoid advertising competing services or companies in messages, usernames, or profile pictures.  
- **Sustained disruptions**: Repeated interference with the community’s functioning or goals.  
- **Threats or violence**: Including any form of violent language.  

---

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement  
- **Reporting Violations**: If you witness or experience a violation of this policy, please contact any chat administrator directly. All reports will be treated confidentially unless disclosure is required by law.  
- **Consequences**: Organizers may take appropriate action for policy violations, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the community.  

---

## 5. Disclaimer  
All information and advice provided in this chat are based on personal experience or knowledge and may not always be accurate. Users are responsible for validating and implementing any guidance provided.
